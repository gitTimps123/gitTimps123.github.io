Webhook Relay Service

HMAC-verified webhook intake with retries & delivery logs

Webhooks fail quietly — endpoints go down, signatures get checked wrong, events get lost. This service receives webhooks, verifies HMAC signatures, fans deliveries out to subscriber endpoints, retries failures with exponential backoff, and keeps a full delivery log so nothing disappears silently.
